## Prompt de génération

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: portrait illustration for the right-hand panel of the GameStackr multi-TCG login page, not a full webpage mockup.
Create a premium collectible-card still life: three beautifully illustrated original fantasy trading cards in a slightly overlapping fan, large and clearly readable as collectible objects. One central blue-framed card with a luminous silver-and-blue dragon, a violet-framed celestial spellcaster card behind on the left, an emerald forest creature card behind on the right. Use original creatures, no existing game logos or recognizable copyrighted card layouts. Ornate but restrained foil borders, tiny graphic symbols and abstract decorative bars instead of words, realistic glossy card stock and iridescent edges. The central card is nearly upright and occupies about 65 percent of the image height; side cards offset behind it. Atmospheric midnight navy background with electric blue and subtle violet light trails, a few floating dust motes, richly detailed card illustrations, cinematic studio lighting. Elegant, tactile, collection-focused, sharp and polished, not childish. Portrait 3:4 composition, cards centered with breathing room around their edges and darker negative space at the bottom for a short UI caption to be added in HTML. No text, letters, typography, watermark, website UI, people or hands. Full opaque background.
